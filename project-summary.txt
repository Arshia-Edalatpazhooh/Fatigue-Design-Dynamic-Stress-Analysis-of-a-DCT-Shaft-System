This project presents the comprehensive fatigue design and structural sizing of concentric transmission shafts for a high-performance 254-horsepower automotive dual-clutch transmission (DCT) delivering a peak engine torque of 390 Newton-meters. Static equilibrium and Macaulay bracket singularity functions were employed to construct shear force and bending moment distributions across three-dimensional orthogonal loading planes for both the inner solid shaft and outer hollow shaft under first and second gear engagements. High-cycle fatigue analysis was executed using the Westinghouse and ASME-elliptic failure criteria implemented via custom Python and MATLAB numerical solvers, incorporating Marin surface, size, and 99.99% reliability endurance limit modifications alongside Peterson stress concentration factors for shoulders, retaining ring grooves, and end-milled keyways. Both shafts were engineered from hot-rolled AISI 1045 steel with an ultimate tensile strength of 570 MPa, utilizing a design factor of safety of 3.0 to withstand extreme powertrain torque shock loads and cyclic rotating bending reversals. For the solid inner shaft, calculated minimum fatigue diameters ranged from 11.37 mm at the lightly loaded bearing seat to 50.64 mm at the critical first-gear shoulder, which were subsequently standardized into stepped diameters of 45, 48, 52, 55, and 68 mm to match standard deep-groove ball bearing bores. For the concentric hollow outer shaft, an iterative trial-and-error solver determined diameter ratios converging to an inner clearance bore of 86 mm and final stepped outer diameters of 110 and 118 mm, satisfying both internal bearing packaging and torsional rigidity constraints. Rectangular driving keys made of cold-drawn AISI 1020 steel were sized against compressive crushing with standardized 40-millimeter lengths and snap-ring axial retention, while full assembly feasibility was validated through standardized tolerance fits. The resulting engineering drawings and CAD models provide a fully validated, production-ready dual-clutch transmission architecture optimized for low manufacturing cost, extended fatigue life, and seamless automated gear shifting.
